Ndabish@gmail.com account and linked from tryregenesys.com.

This file holds the instrument, not any answers. Raw responses are never
committed to this repo; only anonymized aggregates go in /exports.

Reconciled against the live form on Oct 31, 2026 (read-only review; no
answers entered, nothing submitted, form cleared afterward). The form as
built presents 20 fields: 17 questions plus 3 follow-up fields — the
"What happened" text (field 5), the "If yes, why?" text (field 8), and the
research-call email (field 20). Required fields are field 1 and field 14
only. The 1–10 scales show no endpoint labels on the live form.

Consent opener (shown on the form): Regenesys is in pre-launch and this isn't
medical advice or a medical intake. Answers are used for product research and
may be reported to partners/investors only as anonymized totals. Please don't
share medical details. This takes about 3 minutes, and joining the waitlist
does NOT require this form.

Design goal: evidence accelerators and VCs actually screen for — problem
severity, current spend, switching behavior, willingness to pay, feature
priorities, early-adopter intent — plus product co-creation. NO diagnoses,
medications, lab values, or medical history are collected. Anonymous by
default; contact details are asked only in the final, separate opt-in.

## A. Problem validation
1. Have you used an online telehealth service in the past 12 months? (Yes/No, required)
2. What did you use it for? (checkboxes: Weight management / Hormone health /
   Longevity & wellness / Primary care / Mental health / Other)
3. How often? (Once / A few times / Monthly / Ongoing subscription)
4. What's your #1 frustration with it? (dropdown: Rushed visits / Different
   clinician every time / Surprise charges or subscription traps / Support
   never answers / Impersonal, cookie-cutter plans / Other)
5. Tell us what happened (no medical details, please) (text field — follow-up to Q4)
6. How big a problem is that for you? (1–10 scale, no endpoint labels shown)
7. Have you ever quit a telehealth service? (Yes/No)
8. If yes, why? (text field — follow-up to Q7)

## B. Money & switching (market evidence)
9. What do you currently spend per month on telehealth/wellness
   subscriptions? ($0 / $1–50 / $51–100 / $101–200 / $200+)
10. If a service fixed your #1 frustration, what would you fairly pay monthly?
    ($0 / up to $50 / $51–100 / $101–150 / $150+)
11. How likely are you to switch providers in the next 6 months? (1–10 scale,
    no endpoint labels shown)

## C. Dream product (co-creation)
12. Rank what matters most (pick top 3): Same care team every time / Fast,
    human messaging / Plans built around my labs / Transparent flat pricing /
    A care coordinator who checks in / Community of people like me / Easy
    scheduling
13. "Design your dream telehealth company — what would it have that nobody
    offers today?" (open text)

## D. Who you are (segment evidence, all optional except age confirmation)
14. I am 18 or older (required checkbox)
15. Age band (18–29 / 30–44 / 45–59 / 60+)
16. State (text field)
17. How did you hear about Regenesys? (Instagram / TikTok / Friend / Search /
    Other)

## E. Intent & follow-up (separate opt-in)
18. Would you join the founding community and give feedback as we build?
    (Yes/No)
19. Open to a 15-minute research call? (Yes/No)
20. Email (only if you're open to a research call) (text field — follow-up
    to Q19; stored with the call list, not attached to published data)
